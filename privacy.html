Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

This policy explains what data the AstroSiddh mobile app (“the app”, Android package com.astrosiddh) collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it. The app is published by Rahul Sharma (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, email info@astrosiddh.com.

Summary

  • We collect only what the app needs to give you readings: the profile and birth details you enter, your palm photos, your chat messages and your app settings.
  • We do not sell your data, show you ads based on it, or share it with advertisers.
  • The app contains no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

Your account

When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous account for you automatically. You do not need to sign up. This account is identified by a random ID and lets the app keep your profile, readings and credits on our servers. The sign-in keys for this account are stored securely on your phone (in the Android Keystore).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS or files from your phone.

Permissions

Camera — used only to take a photo of your palm for palm reading. The app asks for this permission when you first open the palm reading feature, and you can refuse it. The rest of the app works without it.

Internet — used to fetch your readings from our servers.

How your readings are made

Readings come from our own library of astrology content, written in English, Hindi and Hinglish. Your data is not sent to any third-party AI or astrology service.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only:

  • with the hosting provider that runs our servers, who processes it only on our behalf;
  • when the law requires it, for example a valid order from a court or government authority.

Some profile pictures shown in the app load from a public avatar service (DiceBear). These requests do not include any of your personal data.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on secured servers. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords, if you ever set one, are stored only as a secure hash. Access to the servers is limited to people who need it to run the service.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, birth details, palm photos, chat history, reports and credits are deleted from our servers immediately. Copies in server backups are removed within 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app at any time, and delete your account and data as described above. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and other applicable laws, you can also ask us for a summary of the data we hold about you, or to correct it. Email info@astrosiddh.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
