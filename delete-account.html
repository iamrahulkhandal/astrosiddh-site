Delete your AstroSiddh account

AstroSiddh by Rahul Sharma

You can permanently delete your AstroSiddh account and all data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the AstroSiddh app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted from our servers straight away, and the app starts fresh.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject “Delete my AstroSiddh account”. Include the name, email address or phone number you entered in your profile, so we can find your account.

We will confirm and delete your account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number and gender
  • Your birth date, time and place
  • Your palm photos and palm readings
  • Your chat history
  • Your unlocked reports and credit balance
  • Your app settings

Deletion is permanent. Unused credits and unlocked reports cannot be restored.

What we keep

Copies in server backups are removed within 30 days. Server logs (IP address and request time, with no profile data) are kept for up to 90 days for security, then deleted.
